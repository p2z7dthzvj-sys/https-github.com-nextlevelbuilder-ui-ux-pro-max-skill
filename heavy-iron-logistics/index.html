Fleet & equipment

The right trailer for every load

Diverse trailing capacity for non-standard, high-weight loads. Our team specs the configuration by weight, height and route.

Photo slot

Removable Gooseneck (RGN)

Drive-on

The front detaches to form a ramp so wheeled or tracked machinery drives itself on. The workhorse for excavators, dozers and heavy loaders.

Photo slot

Lowboys

Tall loads

A deck that rides low to the ground, lowering overall height so tall and oversized loads travel legally under bridges and power lines.

Photo slot

Step Decks / Drop Decks

Versatile

A stepped deck gains usable height without losing load length — right for mid-heavy equipment that overheights a standard flatbed.

Photo slot

Standard Flatbeds

General freight

For conventional industrial freight, materials and legal-dimension components — professionally strapped, chained and tarped.
