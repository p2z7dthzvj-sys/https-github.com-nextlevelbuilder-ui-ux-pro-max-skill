About Us

Your Partner for
Demanding Hauls

Heavy Iron Logistics is a Mission, Texas-based specialized freight carrier operating at the US–Mexico border. We combine deep local knowledge of cross-border logistics with the expertise needed to move the heaviest and most complex loads.

Licensed under DOT and MC authority, we operate as both a direct carrier and freight forwarder. Our customs brokerage partnerships ensure seamless import and export documentation for every shipment — no matter how heavy, wide, or tall.
