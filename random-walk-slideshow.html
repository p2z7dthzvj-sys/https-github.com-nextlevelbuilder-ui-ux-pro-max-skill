Wall Street sells
a beautiful promise.

That the market can be beaten. That somewhere in the noise — the charts, the tickers, the breaking news — there is a signal. A pattern. A way to win.

Act I  —  The Signal

Act II  —  The Random Walk

Every step is a coin toss.

Today's price already reflects everything the world currently knows. Tomorrow's move depends on tomorrow's surprises — and surprises, by definition, can't be predicted.

Act III  —  The Flaw in Us

"This time
is different."

It never is.

The flaw is not in the math — it's in us. The fear of missing out. The comfort of the crowd. The certainty, right up until the fall, that the rules have changed.

Act IV  —  The Quiet Answer

Don't try to beat
the market.

"Buy it."

Instead of betting on one stock, or one genius — buy a tiny slice of everything through a simple, low-cost index fund. You give up the dream of spectacular wins. In return, you stop paying for advice that doesn't work.
